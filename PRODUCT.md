# PRODUCT.md: amagilabs.github.io

Written 2026-10-28 from the build brief and the live GitHub org. Assumptions are marked **[assumed]**.

## What it is
The public index of AmagiLabs, the open-source lab of Amagisolutionsgroup, Inc. (GitHub org: amagilabs).
It lists every public repo: open-source command-line tools and Claude Code skills, each MIT licensed,
written in Python, installed with `pipx`.

## Who it is for
- Developers and technical operators at small businesses and law firms who found one tool and want to see the rest. **[assumed]**
- People arriving from a repo README, a social post or a search for a specific job ("receipts to CSV", "lease renewal deadlines"). **[assumed]**

## What the page must get them to do
Find the tool for their job and copy its install line. Secondary: open the repo on GitHub, follow the org.

## Surface and mode
One static page on GitHub Pages, no build step on GitHub's side. Mode: **Persuade** (a landing page for a catalogue).
The list and the type-to-find box carry the page; the rest is short.

## Brand (fixed)
- Colours: cream #F6F4F1, ink #161616, terracotta #D27A73, deep terracotta #B4544B, sand #E6C89C.
- Type: Montserrat (text), Playfair Display (display). Self-hosted, Latin subset.
- Marks: the flask-and-clouds icon and the AMAGI LABS lockup from `amagilabs_logo/final`.
- Voice: "Real builds. No hype." Plain, specific, no inflated claims.

## Hard limits
- Only facts that can be grounded in the repos, their READMEs and the org profile. No pricing, statistics, testimonials or customer names.
- No analytics or third-party scripts. **[assumed]**
- Light and dark aware; WCAG AA contrast; works without JavaScript (the list is prerendered).
